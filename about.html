Our Mission

Malaysian Food Diary was created to be more than just a recipe blog. Our mission is to document the cultural heritage of Malaysia one dish at a time. We believe that food is the universal language that connects the diverse ethnicities of Malaysia—Malay, Chinese, Indian, and the indigenous groups of Borneo.

Why We Do This

In a fast-paced world, traditional cooking methods are often lost. This platform serves as a digital archive for the soul of Malaysian cooking. From the smoky streets of Penang to the family kitchens in Kuala Lumpur, we bring the authentic taste of Malaysia to your screen.
