Nasi Lemak Recipe

Ingredients

2 cups Jasmine rice, 1.5 cups Coconut milk, Pandan leaves, Dried anchovies, Peanuts, Sambal paste...

Instructions

1. Cook rice with coconut milk and pandan. 2. Fry anchovies until crispy. 3. Serve with sambal and boiled egg.
